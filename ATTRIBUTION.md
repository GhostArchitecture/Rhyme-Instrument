# Data sources

- **CMUdict** (Carnegie Mellon Pronouncing Dictionary) — BSD-style license. Compiled to `cmu_skel.json`.
- **SUBTLEX-US** word frequencies — Brysbaert, M., & New, B. (2009). *Moving beyond Kučera and Francis: A critical evaluation of current word frequency norms and the introduction of a new and improved word frequency measure for American English.* Behavior Research Methods, 41, 977–990. Freely available data; used here as a ranked word list (`subtlex_rank.txt`), via the `subtlex-word-frequencies` package (ISC).
